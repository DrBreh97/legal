Privacy Policy

What we collect

Your name, email, optional phone number, countries and dates logged, photos processed for stickers/plans, itineraries, an anonymized hardware device identifier (used strictly to manage trial eligibility and prevent fraud), and device location data (when permitted).

Photos & location

Photo location data (EXIF GPS) is read on your device to match photos to countries. Your camera roll index stays on your phone. Only photos you choose to upload leave the device. Device location data is used locally to detect international border crossings and trigger travel arrival notifications, and is never sold, traded, or shared with third parties for advertising.

Referral & Attribution Tracking Data

To operate our referral reward system, we collect and process unique identifiers (such as custom user referral codes and deep link tracking parameters). This data is linked to your account solely for the purpose of attributing successful sign-ups, preventing self-referral abuse, verifying device/IP uniqueness, and granting accrued rewards. We do not sell or trade referral tracking data to third parties.

How we use it

To run your album, passport, planner and leaderboard, to verify stickers, to generate trip suggestions, and to trigger arrival notifications. We do not sell your personal data.

Sharing

Trips are only visible to friends you invite. Leaderboards show your name, title and counts to your friends.

Service providers

We use trusted providers for hosting, file storage, automated image & text processing and in-app purchases (RevenueCat, Apple, Google). They process data only to provide their service.

Your choices

Deleting your account in Settings immediately and permanently removes your profile, photo indexes, and saved itineraries from our databases. You can also disable location permissions at any time in your device settings.

Questions about privacy? Email support@worldfolio.app.
